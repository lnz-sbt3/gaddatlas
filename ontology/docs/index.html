Description

  • An ontology for modelling narrative spatiality in Carlo Emilio Gadda's "Quer pasticciaccio brutto de via Merulana", integrating place references, critical spatial interpretations, and geographic anchoring across a three-tier data model.

  • GaddAtlas reuses terms from SKOS, PROV-O, GeoSPARQL, and Web Annotation without importing the complete ontologies, to maintain lightweight reasoning and avoid unintended inferences.

Gazetteer Entity c

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#GazetteerEntity
Description

  • GazetteerEntity does not belong to the world of the text: it models the external topographic counterpart. The relation to a NarrativePlace is not assumed as identity but as a typed anchoring, asserted by SpatialInterpretation.

  • GazetteerEntity non appartiene al mondo del testo: modella la controparte topografica esterna. La relazione con NarrativePlace non è assunta come identità, ma come ancoraggio tipizzato, espresso da SpatialInterpretation.

  • An extra-textual georeferenceable place described as an authority/gazetteer entity, typically endowed with stable geometry (coordinates or GeoJSON) and persistent identifiers; it provides the external topographic referent to which narrative places may be anchored.

  • Luogo extra-testuale georeferenziabile, descritto come entità di authority/gazetteer e tipicamente dotato di geometria stabile (coordinate o GeoJSON) e identificatori persistenti; costituisce il referente topografico esterno a cui possono essere ancorati i luoghi narrativi.

Narrative Place c

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#NarrativePlace
Description

  • NarrativePlace include sia luoghi importati o riconducibili a toponimi reali, sia luoghi inventati ma diegeticamente localizzati (es. edifici finzionali collocati in una via reale). L’eventuale corrispondenza topografica è dichiarata tramite ancoraggio a GazetteerEntity e qualificata da status/relazioni.

  • NarrativePlace includes both imported places traceable to real toponyms and invented places that are diegetically localized (e.g., fictional buildings located on a real street). Any topographic correspondence is declared via anchoring to a GazetteerEntity and qualified by status/relations.

  • NarrativePlace modella l’unità spaziale “nel romanzo”, distinta dal referente topografico esterno (GazetteerEntity). La relazione fra i due piani è espressa tramite ancoraggi e relazioni tipizzate a livello di SpatialInterpretation.

  • NarrativePlace models the spatial unit “in the novel”, distinct from the external topographic referent (GazetteerEntity). The relation between the two planes is expressed through anchoring and typed relations at the level of SpatialInterpretation.

  • A spatial entity belonging to the world of the text: a place constructed and recognizable within the diegesis, independently of any correspondence with an extra-textual georeferenceable place.

  • Entità spaziale appartenente al mondo del testo: un luogo costruito e riconoscibile nella diegesi, indipendentemente dalla sua eventuale corrispondenza con un luogo extra-testuale georeferenziabile.

Narrative Route c

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#NarrativeRoute
Description

  • A route (tragitto): an ordered aggregate of narrative places traversed by a focalizer. Distinct from NarrativePlace — a route is not a place but a second-order sequence of places. Carries its own interpretations (per focalizer) via SpatialInterpretation, and aggregates ordered nodes via isNodeOfRoute / routeOrder.

  • A second-order spatial entity representing an ordered path connecting multiple narrative places, as perceived by one or more focalizers.

  • Entità spaziale di secondo livello che rappresenta un percorso ordinato che connette più luoghi narrativi, così come percepito da uno o più focalizzatori.
In Range Of
  • is node of route op
  • targets route op

Place Reference c

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#PlaceReference
Description

  • A PlaceReference does not “resolve” a referent nor determine the place type: disambiguation, classification, and any topographic anchoring are asserted at the level of SpatialInterpretation.

  • PlaceReference non “risolve” il referente né determina il tipo di luogo: la disambiguazione, la classificazione e l’eventuale ancoraggio topografico sono esplicitati a livello di SpatialInterpretation.

  • A textual occurrence in which the text mentions or evokes a spatial element; it is the basic evidential unit and includes bibliographic and contextual information (e.g., source, page, passage, quotation).

  • Occorrenza testuale in cui il testo menziona o richiama un elemento spaziale; costituisce l’unità attestativa di base e include informazioni bibliografiche e contestuali (es. fonte, pagina, segmento, citazione).

Spatial Interpretation c

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#SpatialInterpretation
Description

  • SpatialInterpretation esplicita la natura dichiarativa e tracciabile dell’annotazione: diverse interpretazioni possono coesistere per lo stesso PlaceReference, con diverse assunzioni e livelli di confidenza; per questo il modello privilegia proprietà “targets/assigns/anchors” a questo livello.

  • SpatialInterpretation makes the declarative and traceable nature of annotation explicit: multiple interpretations may coexist for the same PlaceReference, with different assumptions and confidence levels; therefore the model privileges “targets/assigns/anchors” properties at this level.

  • An interpretive act that operates on a PlaceReference and asserts (i) the diegetic place it activates (target), (ii) semantic qualifications via controlled vocabularies (e.g., narrative role, reality status, degree of spatial determination, spatial relation type), and (iii) any anchoring to an external topographic referent (gazetteer).

  • Atto interpretativo che prende in carico un PlaceReference e asserisce (i) quale luogo diegetico esso attiva (target), (ii) le qualificazioni semantiche tramite vocabolari controllati (es. ruolo narrativo, status di realtà, grado di determinazione spaziale, tipo di relazione spaziale), e (iii) l’eventuale ancoraggio a un referente topografico esterno (gazetteer).

Description

Declares that a spatial interpretation is a node (stop) of a narrative route. The node's place is reached via targetsPlace; its position via routeOrder; its focalizer via hasFocalizer. The route of a node and the node share the same focalizer.
Domain SpatialInterpretation c
Range Narrative Route c

is part of op

IRI https://w3id.org/chora#isPartOf
Description

Meronymic relation between narrative places: the subject place is a structural component of the object place (e.g. a staircase of a building). Invariant with respect to focalizer. A place with isPartOf inherits its geographic anchoring from the parent and should NOT carry its own anchorsToEntity.
Domain NarrativePlace c
Range NarrativePlace c
